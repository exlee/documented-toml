### A recorded default is TOML, so it names its key the way the line under it
### does. A key written dotted keeps every segment the `[table]` header above
### does not supply, and uncommenting the line sets the key it is about.
--- DEF ---
##: How long to wait.
a.b = 1
--- USR ---
a.b = 2
--- RES ---
##: How long to wait.
#: a.b = 1
a.b = 2
### The person's own spelling decides it. Written under a header, the header
### supplies the prefix and the line names the leaf alone.
--- USR ---
[a]
b = 2
--- RES ---
[a]
##: How long to wait.
#: b = 1
b = 2
