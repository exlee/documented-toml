### A block whose first `#:` line names a key documents that key, even where
### the defaults ship no value for it. That name is the block's anchor: once
### the person declares the key for real, the block travels to sit above what
### they wrote, and the key is not reported as one the defaults do not know.
--- DEF ---
##: The only declared option.
declared = 1

##: Accounts are written as [[accounts]] blocks:
##:
#: [[accounts]]
#: name = "Personal"
#: host = "imap.example.com"
