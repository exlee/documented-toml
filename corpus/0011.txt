### A line is the tool's when its first non-whitespace characters are exactly
### the marker and its run of hashes is no longer than the marker's. With the
### default marker, `#:` is the tool's; `##:` and `#` are the person's.
--- DEF ---
#: Documented.
value = 1
--- USR ---
##: this looks like a marker but is not
#not spaced
#:also the tool's, so it goes
value = 2
--- RES ---
#: Documented.
#: value = 1
##: this looks like a marker but is not
#not spaced
value = 2
