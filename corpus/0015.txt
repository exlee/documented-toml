### The blank line the defaults put between two documented options is part of
### the shape they give the file, so a person whose file has no blank there
### gets one. Without it, options with a paragraph of prose each run together.
--- DEF ---
##: The first option, with a paragraph of its own.
first = 1

##: The second, kept apart from the first.
second = 2
--- USR ---
first = 9
second = 8
--- RES ---
##: The first option, with a paragraph of its own.
#: first = 1
first = 9

##: The second, kept apart from the first.
#: second = 2
second = 8
### Blank lines the person did write are theirs, and are preserved exactly.
--- USR ---
first = 9


second = 8

#: first = 1
first = 9
